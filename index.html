PHYSICS

Physics is a natural science that seeks to understand how the universe works at every scale—from the smallest particles to the largest cosmic structures. It examines matter, energy, forces, and the interactions between them. At its core, physics aims to identify universal principles that explain natural phenomena, often expressed through mathematical laws.
